Carbide Insert MOQ: Ordering 10 Pieces per Model

For the standard carbide turning inserts covered below, CarbideAxis's minimum order quantity is 10 pieces per model — one standard box. The same minimum applies to trial orders. Different models cannot be combined to meet one model's minimum. Standard solid carbide end mills have a separate minimum: 1 piece per model.

Standard solid carbide end mills have a separate minimum: 1 piece per model.

What “per model” means for your order

Your requested quantity Meets the minimum?
10 pieces of CNMG120408 Yes
10 pieces of CNMG120408 + 10 pieces of WNMG080408 Yes: 20 pieces in total
5 pieces of CNMG120408 + 5 pieces of WNMG080408 No: each model is below its 10-piece minimum

Our standard insert packaging contains 10 pieces of one model per plastic box. You can include several models in one order, with each model meeting its own minimum. Neutral packaging is available on request.

Which models does this guide cover?

The following turning insert models are in our regular stock range:

CNMG120408, WNMG080408, CNMG120404, TNMG160408, VNMG160408, CCMT09T308, CCMT09T304, DNMG150608, CCMT060204, DCMT11T308, SNMG120408 and WNMG080404.

Regular stock range does not guarantee that every requested specification and quantity is available immediately. Send the full model code, any grade or chipbreaker reference on your current box, and the quantity you need so we can check availability.

For other models, PCD inserts, CBN inserts and cermet inserts, ask us to confirm the MOQ before ordering. Non-standard inserts can be quoted from a drawing or sample, with quantity, cost and lead time confirmed for the specific request.

Check the specification before placing a trial order

If you have the full insert code, include it with your request. If the code is missing or unclear, send clear photos of the insert, its packaging label and the holder code or holder pocket.

Our insert identification service can help with preliminary identification. Photos alone cannot confirm the grade, coating or complete compatibility. Dimensions and holder information may also be needed before a match can be checked.

A 10-piece trial lets you test the proposed insert on your application. It does not guarantee a particular tool life or establish that a trial is large enough for every job. Agree on the material, operation and acceptance criteria, then record acceptable parts per edge, dimensional stability and surface finish. See the cost-per-edge guide for a comparison method.

Sample charges and lead time

Samples are charged at actual cost. Sample charges may be credited against a qualifying first order; ask for the qualifying amount and credit conditions in your quotation before ordering.

For your trial request, send the model, quantity and destination. Ask the quotation to state the product cost, shipping charge, availability, lead time and any other applicable charges. Confirm what the lead time covers and keep it separate from the carrier's estimated transit time.

The supply range and ordering information brings our general ordering terms together. Availability and the schedule for your specific order are confirmed with the quotation.

Payment and shipping

Payment is by T/T; payment details are confirmed with the quotation. Shipping options include FedEx, UPS and DHL. Send your destination so we can confirm the available service, shipping charge and estimated transit time for your order.

What to send for a trial quotation

  1. Model and quantity: one line per model, with at least 10 pieces for each of the covered turning models.
  2. Material and operation: for example, the workpiece material and whether the job involves roughing, semi-finishing or finishing.
  3. Existing insert and holder details: full codes or clear photos, especially when requesting a replacement.
  4. Delivery destination and reply details: so availability, shipping and the quotation can be confirmed with you.

If you are unsure about the model, start with the information you have. The workpiece material guide and insert size chart can help you prepare your request.

Frequently asked questions

Can I combine different models to reach 10 pieces?

Each covered model has its own 10-piece minimum. An order containing 10 pieces of CNMG120408 and 10 pieces of WNMG080408 contains 20 pieces in total.

Can I buy one or two inserts to check the fit?

The minimum for the covered turning models is 10 pieces, including trial orders. Before ordering, send the insert code, holder details and photos for a preliminary check. Additional measurements may be needed to assess compatibility.

Does the 10-piece minimum apply to end mills?

No. Standard solid carbide end mills start at 1 piece per model. Ask about other product types and non-standard tools separately.

Is the sample charge refundable?

The confirmed policy is a possible credit against a qualifying first order. A credit is different from a cash refund. Ask us to confirm the credit conditions and qualifying amount before placing the sample order.

Can I order private-label inserts in a 10-piece trial quantity?

Private label for the covered insert range starts at 2,000 pieces per model. Standard or neutral packaging is available for the 10-piece trial quantity.

Can you quote shipping for a single box?

Yes. Send the model and destination with your 10-piece trial request so we can check the shipping service and quote the charge.
