RFQ FAQ

Short answers to common questions before buyers send a quotation request.

What is the MOQ for carbide inserts?

Insert MOQ is 10 pieces per model (one box); models cannot be mixed to reach the minimum. All standard solid carbide end mill models start from 1 pc.

Can you provide neutral or private label packaging?

Yes. Neutral packing is available at standard quantities, and private label packing starts from 2,000 pieces per model.

How should I request a quote?

Send the ISO code, drawing, sample photo, model list, workpiece material, quantity and current cutting problem.

Can you match a long model list?

Yes. Send Excel, PDF, screenshots or package labels, and we will check available models by product family.
