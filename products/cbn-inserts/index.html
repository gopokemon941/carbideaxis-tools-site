CBN selection depends on the workpiece

Confirm material hardness, grade and continuous or interrupted cutting conditions. CBN is widely used for hardened-steel finishing; some grades also suit gray cast iron. Recommendations must be tied to the actual application.

Typical supply

CBN turning inserts, CBN grooving tools, solid CBN and brazed CBN inserts

What to send

Hardness HRC, operation, interrupted/continuous cut, toolholder and current failure mode.
