Start with your workpiece and operation

Know the job but not the code? Start from the material you cut. Each group below names the problem that group causes, the models buyers usually check first, and the guide section that explains the choice.

P — Steel

General steels and alloy steels. The starting point for most external turning: a medium chipbreaker on an 80° insert covers roughing to semi-finishing. Buyers usually check CNMG120408 or WNMG080408 first, and SNMG120408 for heavy roughing.

How to choose for steel →

M — Stainless steel

Stainless sticks, work-hardens and builds up on the edge. A sharper chipbreaker class and steady chip control matter more than a harder edge. Positive inserts such as CCMT09T308 are common on smaller lathes and boring work.

How to choose for stainless →

K — Cast iron

Short chips, abrasive wear and often an interrupted surface. Toughness and edge support come first: CNMG120408 and SNMG120408 are the usual starting points, confirmed against your surface condition.

How to choose for cast iron →

Working on aluminium, heat-resistant alloys or hardened steel instead? See all six material groups (P, M, K, N, S, H). Choosing between the two 80° workhorses? Read the CNMG vs WNMG comparison before you change holder systems.

Turning insert FAQ

What is the MOQ for turning inserts?

10 pieces per model — one standard box. Models cannot be mixed to reach the minimum. Full ordering terms are on the supply range page.

Can I start with a trial before a stock order?

Yes. A trial starts from 10 pieces of one model, so you can score it on your own machine and material before committing. Sample and trial quantities typically take 7–15 days; the confirmed date is in your quotation.

Can you supply inserts under our own label?

Yes — neutral packing is available at standard quantities, and private label packing starts from 2,000 pieces per model.

Which material group is stainless steel?

Stainless is group M. Start from the stainless section of the material groups guide, then confirm the chipbreaker class against your operation — stainless usually needs the sharper version of the same model.

The code on my insert has worn off. Can you still match it?

Yes. Send clear photos through the photo identification service and we will identify the most likely ISO code with you before quoting.

Send the complete codes and quantities you have. Add the material, holder or cutter details when available so we can check the right supply options.
