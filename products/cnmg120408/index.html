CNMG120408 Carbide Turning Insert

The 80° negative workhorse for external turning of steel, stainless steel and cast iron. This page gives the exact ISO dimensions, the standard chipbreaker classes and the trial terms — send the code and grade on your current box and we confirm the match for your holder and workpiece before quoting.

Quick facts

ISO / ANSI

CNMG120408 · CNMG432

Shape

Rhombic 80°, negative, double-sided — 4 usable edges

Size

IC 12.7 mm · thickness 4.76 mm · hole 5.16 mm · nose radius 0.8 mm

Packing

10 pieces per plastic box

The code reads position by position — shape, clearance, tolerance, size, thickness, nose radius. If you are new to reading it, the insert code guide decodes CNMG120408 letter by letter.

Chipbreaker class and material — confirmed for your job, not guessed

For steel (group P), buyers usually start from a medium chipbreaker class; for stainless steel (group M), a sharper class is usually checked first; for cast iron (group K), edge support comes first. The exact chipbreaker suffix and grade for your job are confirmed manually against your workpiece material and operation before we quote — send the code and grade on your current box, or a photo of it, and the match is checked by a person, not picked from a table.

That is also why a shape match alone is not a promise of equal cutting performance: the ISO code guarantees holder fit, while the chipbreaker and grade decide how the insert behaves in your material. Start from your group in the material groups guide, or compare the 6-edge alternative in CNMG vs WNMG before changing holder systems.

CNMG120408 FAQ

Does CNMG120408 fit my holder?

It fits standard holders made for the CNMG 12 size (the common 95° external turning holders). Send your holder model and we confirm the seat before quoting — a similar-looking insert in the wrong seat cannot be evaluated fairly.

CNMG120408 or WNMG080408?

Same 80° corner, different shape and holder system: CNMG gives 4 usable edges, WNMG gives 6 in its own holders. The full comparison is in CNMG vs WNMG.

What does one trial cost me in commitment?

One box of 10 pieces of one model. There is no requirement to combine models or to commit to a stock quantity before you have scored the trial.

My current insert is a different brand. Can you match it?

Yes — send the full code from the box label, or use the photo identification service if the code has worn off. The replacement is confirmed against your holder and material before quoting.
